# CineWatch Engineering Glossary

- **Authority** - the source permitted to decide a fact within a defined domain.
- **Projection** - deterministic human- or machine-readable output derived from a canonical source.
- **Qualification** - evidence that a candidate passed the checks required for its scope.
- **Chronicle** - generated Markdown view of the append-only engineering event ledger.
- **Training eligible** - source content approved by policy for inclusion in a training/retrieval corpus.
- **Training review required** - source content that must be reviewed at record level before training use.
- **Reference only** - indexed for provenance but excluded from training text to avoid duplication, licensing risk, or generated noise.
- **NexVox projection commit** - a commit whose purpose is to persist a corpus projection for an exact preceding source commit.
- **Fail closed** - treat unknown permission as no permission until authority proves otherwise.
- **Semantic HTML** - meaningful document structure rendered through React/Next.js rather than anonymous visual containers.
- **CSS-first** - CineWatch design authority is expressed primarily through semantic HTML, design tokens, and native CSS; Tailwind remains optional.
